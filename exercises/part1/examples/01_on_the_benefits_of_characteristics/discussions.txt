What is architecture?

It is the structure and interconnection of components and how they
communicate to solve the problem at hand.

But, isn't that part of design as well?

Architecture is a very high level design where the decisions have
a very high impact and cost compared to design decisions.

A good architecture is not the one that correctly predicts the future
but make adapting to the future needs more economical and affordable.

Functional requirements:
  A user may access their toll/travel records

Non-functional requirements:
  Things we tend to think are not significant and the word "non"
  sadly does not help either.

  These are expectations, constraints, and challenges that a system
  should be able to meet.

  At a given time, 10,000 users may need information about their
  travel / toll charges

These are often the ities in systems and a better name may be
characteristically.

Security
Scalability
Accessibility
...
Performance
...
