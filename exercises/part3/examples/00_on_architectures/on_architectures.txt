Monoliths are distributed so then what is the difference
between monoliths and distributed architecture?

Monoliths are about having a single unit of deployment whereas
distributed architectures have multiple units of deployment.

Knowing what characteristic are handled better by an architecture can
help us decide if that architecture is suitable for a requirement for
which we have taken the time to figure out the characteristic.

In the case of monoliths, all the distributed parts have to be 
updated together. In a distributed architecture, the different 
distributed parts may be updated independent of other distributed parts.
