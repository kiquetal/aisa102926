AI is rather inconsistent and non-deterministic

What can we do with that being the case?

1. Use AI more for ideas than the solutions

Ideas have a far less risk than solutions. Solutions require more rigor than ideas

We evaluate multiple ideas, reject a lot of them, consider a few of 
them, and move towards devising solutions for any given problem. The 
ideas may come from humans and also from AI.

2. Consult with at least three AI models to get a consensus on the
ideas and also to be able to get more ideas than one particular model
may (inconsistently/unreliably) provide.

Often many organizations chant "AI first" but I would like to kindly
disagree and emphasis:

"Human first (with time boxed effort) and AI second"

Is AI first Garbage In, Garbage Out (GIGO) for the teams or companies you know?

We as humans should first read, understand, and analyze a problem
to a resonate extent. Don't take way too long, that is not the point.
Please allot a time-boxed effort. This will help us to know enough,
to think about it sufficiently, so when AI generates ideas or
suggestions, we are able to immediately evaluate it rather than assume
what AI creates is good.

MM: AI can't read your thoughts. A draft for code or a text - even if they aren't so good - helps more than a short prompt in my experience.

Even worse, we can't form our thoughts in one shot.
Software exhibits the observer effect. The minute we create something 
and take a look, we have better ideas on how to improve it.

Be iterative in the use of AI. No doubt we are going to get better
at it, but do not aim for a single shot, fastest way to ask AI to
deliver the results.

When starting at a problem:
1. Humans first, take some time boxed effort to understand the problem, and reason about things. This will give us the much needed insight than rushing forward to using AI
2. Use at least three different AI models to ask it for ideas
3. Evaluate the ideas and form a union of thing that are relevant, discard things that seem a extraneous.
